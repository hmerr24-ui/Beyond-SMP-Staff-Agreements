How acceptance works

  1. Open the agreement page linked to you by Beyond Bot and read the complete agreement.
  2. Return to the Discord message and select I Agree.
  3. Enter your exact current Discord username when prompted.
  4. Beyond Bot records your Discord ID, username at signing, rank, agreement version and UTC timestamp.

This website does not collect signatures or personal information. Signing is completed inside the Beyond SMP Discord server.
